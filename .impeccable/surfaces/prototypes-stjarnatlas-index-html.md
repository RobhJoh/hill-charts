# Apogee app shell: start page, Funktionsområde, Projekt with Hill chart

Mode: Operate. Audience: the team in a sync meeting, leaders scanning, an Ägare updating alone on a phone. Constraint: must also work inside a Notion embed (light variant needed). Status: throwaway prototype for the wayfinder ticket "Visual direction and Läge colour scale"; revisions pending from the user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each Projekt is a chart of the night sky: Scopes are stars on a graduated Läge grid, the hill their orbit. Refuses the grey hill-in-a-card SaaS arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy chart ground (#081026/#0b1530), chalk-white type, hairline graticule every 10 Läge, graduated bottom border. Scope colour is a spectral ramp by Läge: orange-red (0) → pale gold (50) → blue-white (100). Dot size = how recently a Scope moved. Red-flashlight red (#e0503a) only for the selected Scope's ring, stuck Scopes and the primary action. Barlow throughout, including the Projekt title (600) and wordmark (700); the user rejected the italic serif heading.

STORY: Visitors grasp the overall shape of the work first, then spot dim, red-ringed Scopes that are stuck, then drag their own Scope.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with Funktionsområden as tabs and actions (Historik, Bädda in, Ny Scope). Left column: the Funktionsområde's Projekt as mini sky charts. Centre: the Projekt title, then the chart filling the work area. Right: Scope index (name, Ägare, Läge, days at Läge) and the colour legend.

FORM: Star atlas chart (catalog challenger notation-diagram-systems-star-atlas), won a bolder re-roll steered toward space; position: chosen over the dealt lead. seed:a4e3799f147bb78a
